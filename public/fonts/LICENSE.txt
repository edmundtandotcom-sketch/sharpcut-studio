SharpCut Studio — burned-in caption fonts
=========================================

The .ttf files in this folder are used by the FFmpeg/libass export pass to
burn captions into the exported MP4. They are all licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL), and were fetched from the Google Fonts
repository (https://github.com/google/fonts, /ofl/<family>/).

Bundled families and upstream source:

  Inter-Bold.ttf                 Inter                — ofl/inter
  Montserrat-Bold.ttf            Montserrat           — ofl/montserrat
  Poppins-SemiBold.ttf           Poppins              — ofl/poppins
  Oswald-SemiBold.ttf            Oswald               — ofl/oswald
  BebasNeue-Regular.ttf          Bebas Neue           — ofl/bebasneue
  BarlowCondensed-SemiBold.ttf   Barlow Condensed     — ofl/barlowcondensed
  ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf       Archivo Black        — ofl/archivoblack
  Nunito-ExtraBold.ttf           Nunito               — ofl/nunito
  PlayfairDisplay-SemiBold.ttf   Playfair Display     — ofl/playfairdisplay
  CourierPrime-Bold.ttf          Courier Prime        — ofl/courierprime

Each family's full OFL text and copyright notice is available in its upstream
directory at github.com/google/fonts. The OFL permits redistribution and
embedding of these fonts. These files are shipped only for local, in-browser
caption rendering; they are not re-sold or offered as a font product.
